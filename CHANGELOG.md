# Changelog

## [26.3.0-2.9.38] - 2026-09-18
Initial version for Minecraft 26.3
### Known Issues
While porting the mod a few issues emerged that I could not yet solve but didn't want to delay release
- Glossary does not render Entities next to their name.  All other glossary content unaffected
- Vanilla foods do not have default XP in Easy or Default packs.  Autovalues may kick in
- Vanilla tools/weapons/armor do not have smithing experience in Easy or Default Packs.  Autovalues may kick in.
