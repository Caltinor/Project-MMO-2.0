# Changelog

## [26.3.0-2.9.40] - 2026-09-30
### Breaking Change Update
NeoForge needed to make some breaking changes in the latest release.  As such, the minimum NF version for pmmo has had to adjust accordingly. There are no feature updates in this release, so if your other mods have not updated yet, you can stay on `pmmo-26.3.0-2.9.39` and NF `26.3.0.22-beta` or even further back `pmmo-26.3.0-2.9.38` and NF `26.3.0.0-beta` if your mods are still on initial release versions.

### Known Issues
While porting the mod a few issues emerged that I could not yet solve but didn't want to delay release
- Glossary does not render Entities next to their name.  All other glossary content unaffected
- Vanilla foods do not have default XP in Easy or Default packs.  Autovalues may kick in
- Vanilla tools/weapons/armor do not have smithing experience in Easy or Default Packs.  Autovalues may kick in.
